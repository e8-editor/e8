# What E8 does

What works today, in a terminal (`e8`) or a window (`e8-gui`). Where it differs from Emacs is in [differences.md](differences.md), and what is still to come in [plan.md](plan.md).

- Buffers.
- Windows (`C-x 2`, `C-x 3`, `C-x o`, `C-x 0`, `C-x 1`, `split-window` and `delete-window` as window.el has them, with atomic windows, `delete-windows-on`, `delete-other-windows-vertically` and `window-toggle-side-windows`, resizing as window.el resizes, with `C-x ^`, `C-x }`, `C-x {`, `C-x +`, `C-x -`, `balance-windows-area`, `fit-window-to-buffer`, `maximize-window`, `minimize-window` and the mouse, preserved and fixed sizes, `display-buffer` as window.el has it, with `display-buffer-alist`, its action functions and side windows, and the `C-x 4` and `C-x 5` commands, `C-M-v`, `C-x <left>` and `C-x <right>` through the buffers a window showed, `quit-window` restoring the window's buffer, place and size as window.el's `quit-restore-window` does, window parameters, dedicated windows, window states (`window-state-get`, `window-state-put`, `window-swap-states`), window configurations with `save-window-excursion`, moving, showing, deleting and swapping by direction with windmove (`windmove-left` and the rest, `windmove-default-keybindings` for `S-<left>` and its kin, `windmove-display-right` and its kin, `windmove-delete-*`, `windmove-swap-states-*`, `windmove-wrap-around`, `windmove-create-window`), and `winner-mode`, with `C-c <left>` and `C-c <right>` undoing and redoing changes to the windows).
- Frames (`C-x 5 2`, `C-x 5 o`, `C-x 5 0` and the other `C-x 5` commands, each frame a native window in the GUI).
- Files (backups, auto-saving and `recover-this-file`, `revert-buffer` and `C-x x g`, `auto-revert-mode`, asking before editing or saving a file changed on disk, DOS and Mac line ends, files in other codings (UTF-16, the ISO 8859 and Windows sets, Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, ISO-2022-JP, GBK, Big5, EUC-KR, found from cookies and the bytes), `C-x RET f`, `C-x RET r` and `C-x RET c`, `describe-coding-system` on `C-h C`, `prefer-coding-system`, `save-some-buffers` and asking about each file on `C-x C-c`, `copy-file`, `rename-file`, `delete-file` to the trash, `make-directory`, `read-only-mode` on `C-x C-q`, `find-alternate-file` on `C-x C-v`, `rename-visited-file`, recent files with `recentf-mode` and `recentf-open`, and `find-file-at-point`, `insert-file` on `C-x i`, `write-region`, `append-to-file`, and lock files, the umask with `default-file-modes` and `with-file-modes`, `verify-visited-file-modtime`, `file-name-completion` and `file-name-all-completions`, `directory-files-and-attributes` and `access-file`, and the primitives files.el builds on: `lock-file` and `lock-buffer`, the `-internal` operations, `file-system-info` and `unix-sync`), gzip and zlib data decompressed in a buffer with `zlib-decompress-region`, and `symbol-file` naming the file that defined a function, variable or face. TLS on network connections, as Emacs's GnuTLS gives it (`gnutls-boot`, `gnutls-peer-status`), started at once or after plain text as STARTTLS does, with `open-network-stream`'s `:type tls` and the Network Security Manager asking about certificates that do not verify or keys that changed, remembered for the session or for good; `secure-hash` and `md5`.
- Dired (`C-x d`, `C-x C-j`: visiting, marking, copying, renaming and deleting files, `g`, `s`, hiding details), with dired-aux's commands: links (`S`, `Y`, `H`), renaming and copying by regexp (`% R`, `% C` and the link ones), `% u` and `% l`, `% g` to mark files by what they hold, `!` and `&` with `*` and `?`, `y`, `M`, `O`, `G`, `T`, `Z` to compress and unpack, `A` to search the marked files into `*xref*` and `Q` to replace in them; dired-x's `dired-omit-mode` (`C-x M-o`) and `F`; and wdired, `C-x C-q`, to rename files and point links elsewhere by editing the listing (`C-c C-c` to finish, `C-c ESC` or `C-c C-k` to take it back).
- Projects (`C-x p f` to find a file in the Git tree, `C-x p p`, `C-x p D`, `C-x p b`, `C-x p k`).
- Questions (`yes-or-no-p`, `y-or-n-p`, and `use-short-answers`) and timers.
- An agent (`M-x agent`: talk to Claude in an Org buffer while it calls commands as tools, asking before it writes files or quits, each turn one undo step).
- Coding agents over the Agent Client Protocol (`M-x agent-shell`, after Emacs's agent-shell: Claude Code through `claude-agent-acp`, Gemini CLI, Codex or Goose, in the same kind of Org buffer, its Markdown replies made Org as they stream in, `C-c RET` to send and `C-c C-k` to stop, its session started as the buffer opens with its model, mode and thought level on the header line (`agent-shell-header-style`, and in variables for one's own mode line), `C-c C-v` to pick the model, `C-c C-t` the thought level, `C-c TAB` or `C-<tab>` the next mode and `C-c C-s` any setting it offers, `C-h h` for its keys, and whether it is working and how full its context is on the mode line; the agent's tool calls, diffs and plan show as it works, each call's output in a src block named by what it holds (diff, console, json or text) folded into the call's line as it comes (TAB opens one, and a call asked about shows its diff while it asks), its thinking a `#+begin_thinking` block folded as any Org block, its tool lines short (commands with their description, MCP tools as `server: tool`, Claude Code's bookkeeping left out) Org's emphasis markers hidden, and the conversation above the request being typed read-only, as a shell's, undo reaching only that request, `M-p` and `M-n` bringing the requests sent before back to the prompt and `M-r` picking one with completion, from one history every agent-shell buffer shares and `savehist-mode` keeps between sessions, earlier sessions in the project resumed as it opens (asked, or the latest with `agent-shell-session-strategy`; Claude Code's own sessions there among them), their conversation replayed into the buffer, every reply but the last folded, and the agent remembering it, the agent's slash commands (Claude Code's own, skills and plugins) completed with TAB or `C-M-i` after a `/` at the start of a request, with what each does in the echo area, an agent that stops started again in the same session, Org's speed commands running anywhere in the read-only transcript (`n`, `p`, `f`, `b`, `u`, `c`, `C`, `o`, `?`), its permission questions are asked when nothing else is asking, files it reads and writes through the editor go through their buffers, and buffers of files it wrote itself are reverted; its login is its own; it also gets E8's own commands, keys and buffers through `e8-mcp`, asking before it saves, discards, quits or runs code in the editor).
- Emacs movement and editing keys (`M-m`, `M-\`, `M-SPC`, `M-^`, `C-x C-o`, `M-z`, `C-q`, `C-S-DEL`, `M-g g`, `C-x =`, `M-=`, `M-@`, `M-h`, `C-M-o`, sentences with `M-a`, `M-e` and `M-k`, `sort-lines`, `reverse-region`, `delete-duplicate-lines`, `keep-lines`, `flush-lines` and `how-many`, registers (`C-x r SPC`, `C-x r j`, `C-x r s`, `C-x r i`) and rectangles (`C-x r k`, `C-x r y`, `C-x r t`), `M-/` (`dabbrev-expand`), abbrevs (`abbrev-mode`, `C-x a g`, `C-x '`), `M-s o` (`occur`), pages (`C-x ]`, `C-x [`, `C-x l`), `C-M-t`, `C-M-h`, `C-x n d`, `C-l` and `M-r` cycling as `recenter-top-bottom` and `move-to-window-line-top-bottom`, the global mark ring (`C-x C-SPC`), `undo-only`, `ESC ESC ESC`, the buffer list (`C-x C-b`, a tabulated list sorted with `S`, titled in the header line), `kill-some-buffers` and `kill-matching-buffers`, highlighting by regexp (`M-s h r`, `M-s h .`), bookmarks (`C-x r m`, `C-x r b`, `C-x r l`, in Emacs's file format), `whitespace-cleanup`, `view-mode` and `view-file`, `M-x diff`, `diff-buffer-with-file` and `diff-mode`, `tabify`, `center-line` and `append-to-buffer`, changing case with `M-u`, `M-l`, `M-c`, `C-x C-u` and `C-x C-l`, paragraphs with `M-{` and `M-}`, defuns with `C-M-a` and `C-M-e` (by the syntax tree in tree-sitter modes), balanced expressions with `C-M-f`, `C-M-b`, `C-M-n`, `C-M-p`, `C-M-u`, `C-M-d`, `C-M-k`, `C-M-SPC` and `M-(`, as each mode's syntax table says).
- Filling (`M-q`, `C-u M-q` to justify, `fill-region`, `C-x f`, `C-x .`, `auto-fill-mode`).
- Matching parens shown (`show-paren-mode`, on by default) and typed in pairs (`electric-pair-mode`).
- Line numbers (`display-line-numbers-mode`, `global-display-line-numbers-mode`).
- History and places kept between sessions (`savehist-mode`, `save-place-mode`).
- A shell in a buffer (`M-x shell`, on comint: `M-p`, `M-n`, `M-r` searching the history with isearch, `C-c M-r`, the shell's history file, `C-c C-c`, `C-c C-o`, the directory followed, passwords read hidden with `read-passwd`).
- Shell commands (`M-!`, `M-|`, `M-&`).
- Going to a definition by name (`M-x imenu`, from the syntax tree in tree-sitter modes, Python's included, and the headings in Org).
- Compiling and grepping in the background (`M-x compile`, `M-x grep`, `recompile`, `C-x p c`) and visiting each message's place (`next-error` on `M-g n`, `M-g p`).
- Finding a regexp in a project (`C-x p g`, into `*xref*`).
- Comments (`M-;`, `C-x C-;`, `C-x ;`, `M-j`).
- Indentation (TAB as the mode says, `C-M-\` through `indent-region-function` where the mode has one, as Org mode's `org-indent-region`, `C-x TAB`, RET and closing braces reindenting in code modes).
- The message log (`C-h e` shows `*Messages*`).
- The active region with shift selection (transient-mark-mode and shift-select-mode, both on).
- Prefix arguments (`C-u`, `M-<digit>`, `C--`).
- Mouse wheel and trackpad scrolling of the window under the pointer.
- Clicks.
- Drags.
- Double and triple clicks and shift-click as Emacs mouse keys (`<mouse-1>`, `<drag-mouse-1>`, `<double-mouse-1>`).
- The pointer's place and moving it (`mouse-position`, `mouse-pixel-position`, `set-mouse-position`), `track-mouse` with `<mouse-movement>` events, the window and the part of it at a place (`window-at`, `coordinates-in-window-p`), and the raw keys of a key sequence (`this-single-command-raw-keys`, `read-key-sequence-vector`).
- Long lines continued by default.
- Who runs E8 and on what, for scripts as Emacs's Lisp asks it (`user-full-name`, `user-login-name`, ids, `system-users`, `num-processors`), and `C-z` and `C-x C-z` (`suspend-frame`) iconifying a window or stopping the terminal for the shell, as `suspend-emacs` does.
- `truncate-lines`, `visual-line-mode` and `global-visual-line-mode`, and horizontal scrolling as Emacs's: long truncated lines scroll sideways to keep point in sight (`auto-hscroll-mode`, `hscroll-margin`, `hscroll-step`), `C-x <` and `C-x >` (`scroll-left`, `scroll-right`), and `window-hscroll` and `set-window-hscroll` for scripts, with `window-vscroll` and `set-window-vscroll`, which the GUI's smooth scrolling sets, `vertical-motion`, `compute-motion`, `line-number-display-width`, `window-line-height`, `window-lines-pixel-dimensions`, `other-window-for-scrolling` and `window-scroll-functions`.
- File-local variables, as Emacs's files.el sets them: a `-*-` line (`-*- mode: org; fill-column: 60 -*-`, on the second line after `#!`) and a Local Variables list near the end of the file, with its prefix and suffix, as `# Local Variables:` ... `# End:`; `mode:` choosing the major mode; safe values set at once and unsafe or risky ones asked about in `*Local Variables*` (`y`, `n`, `!`, `i`), as `enable-local-variables`, `safe-local-variable-values`, the `safe-local-variable` and `risky-local-variable` properties and `enable-local-eval` say; `eval:` for what E8 can run; `file-local-variables-alist`, `hack-local-variables-hook` and `before-hack-local-variables-hook`; and the variables set again whenever the major mode changes. `add-file-local-variable`, `delete-file-local-variable`, `add-file-local-variable-prop-line` and `delete-file-local-variable-prop-line` write them. Directory-local variables from `.dir-locals.el` (and `.dir-locals-2.el`, which overrides it) in the file's directory or one above it, by major mode, derived modes included, and by subdirectory, with `subdirs`, read again when the file changes, asked about as file-local ones are with `+` to trust the directory (`safe-local-variable-directories`), in Dired buffers too, and set for code with `dir-locals-set-class-variables` and `dir-locals-set-directory-class`; `add-dir-local-variable`, `delete-dir-local-variable`, `copy-file-locals-to-dir-locals`, `copy-dir-locals-to-file-locals` and `copy-dir-locals-to-file-locals-prop-line` write them. Scripts read and print Lisp data with `e8.readFromString` and `e8.prin1ToString`.
- Spell checking with aspell, ispell, hunspell or enchant-2, as Emacs's ispell.el: `M-$` (`ispell-word`, corrections in `*Choices*`: a character picks one, `SPC` leaves the word, `i`, `u` and `m` add to your personal dictionary, `a` accepts for the session, `A` in the file's `LocalWords:`, `r` and `R` type a replacement, `R` query-replacing it through the buffer, `l` looks words up in a word list, `x`, `X` and `q` stop, `C-r` edits recursively, `?` helps), `ispell-region`, `ispell-buffer`, `ispell` and `ispell-continue` (`C-u M-$`), skipping URLs, `LocalWords:` lines, HTML markup (`ispell-skip-html`), TeX commands and, with `ispell-check-comments`, comments; `ispell-comments-and-strings`, `ispell-comment-or-string-at-point`, `ispell-complete-word` and word completion at point in Text mode from a word list, `ispell-minor-mode`, `ispell-kill-ispell` and `ispell-pdict-save`. Dictionaries are found as Emacs finds them (`ispell-valid-dictionary-list`) and chosen with `ispell-change-dictionary` (`C-u` for every buffer), `ispell-dictionary`, or a file's own `Local IspellDict:` and `Local IspellPersDict:` lines, or `ispell-local-dictionary` as a file-local or directory-local variable. Several languages at once need Hunspell: set `ispell-program-name` to `hunspell` and `ispell-dictionary` to the dictionaries joined by commas, as `en_US,sv_SE`, and words either knows are right. `flyspell-mode` checks words as you type, underlining misspellings and doubled words; `C-.`/`M-TAB` cycle corrections, `C-;` corrects the misspelling before point, `C-,` goes to the next, `C-c $` offers corrections; `flyspell-prog-mode`, `flyspell-buffer` and `flyspell-region`.
- `subword-mode` (the word commands stop inside CamelCase names) and `superword-mode` (a symbol is one word), and their global modes.
- The kill ring with `M-y` (choosing a kill from the minibuffer when not after a yank).
- The mark ring (`C-u C-SPC`).
- Undo as in Emacs (undoable, with `undo-redo`, text property changes undone, `buffer-undo-list` read, set and bound, `with-silent-modifications`).
- Keyboard macros (`F3`, `F4`, `C-x (`, `C-x )`, `C-x e`).
- Recursive edits (`C-M-c`, `C-]`, `top-level`).
- The keys typed lately (`C-h l`).
- Characters typed with `C-x 8` through `key-translation-map`.
- Keys bound to keys as keyboard macros.
- `sit-for` and `read-event` with a timeout.
- Repeat (`C-x z`).
- Documented variables with buffer-local values (`C-h v`).
- Hooks.
- Major and minor modes with their own keymaps (`C-h m`), and `global-set-key`, `local-set-key`, `global-unset-key` and `local-unset-key`. Scripts reach the current keymaps as values (`currentLocalMap`, `currentGlobalMap`, `useGlobalMap`, `copyKeymap`, `accessibleKeymaps`, `currentMinorModeMaps`, `minorModeKeyBinding`).
- Choosing the major mode from the file name.
- `text-mode`.
- `overwrite-mode` and `delete-selection-mode`.
- Header lines (`header-line-format`).
- Margins (`left-margin-width`, `(margin left-margin)` display specs) and fringes in the GUI (continuation arrows, `(left-fringe BITMAP)` specs, `define-fringe-bitmap`, `indicate-empty-lines`, `indicate-buffer-boundaries`, `fringe-indicator-alist`).
- The overlay arrow (`overlay-arrow-position`, a triangle in the fringe or `=>` on a terminal, marking `next-error`'s message).
- Fonts as values (`font-spec`, `font-get`, `font-xlfd-name`, `face-font`, `font-at`, `font-family-list`, `list-fonts`, `x-list-fonts`, `char-displayable-p`).
- Images (`(image ...)` display specs on text, overlays, strings and in the margins, PNG, JPEG, GIF and SVG, `create-image`, `insert-image`, `put-image`, `insert-sliced-image`, `image-size`, `image-increase-size` and `image-rotate` on `i +` and `i r`, and `image-mode` for visited image files: drawn in the GUI, rows as tall as their images, and shown as their text in the terminal, as Emacs's terminals show them).
- The bell (`ding`, `visible-bell`, the system beep on macOS).
- Text properties.
- Overlays and faces with Emacs's attributes and colour names (`describe-face`, `list-faces-display`, `list-colors-display`, anonymous faces in `face` properties).
- Faces of other sizes and proportional fonts (`:height`, `:family`, `face-remapping-alist`, `C-x C-+`, `C-x C--` and `C-x C-0` for `text-scale-adjust`, `variable-pitch-mode`).
- With the layout's positions for scripts (`posn-at-point`, `pos-visible-in-window-p`, `window-end`, `window-text-pixel-size`).
- Bidirectional text (Hebrew, Arabic and mixed lines reordered as Emacs's bidi.c does, with paired brackets and mirroring, right-to-left paragraphs from the window's right edge, `bidi-paragraph-direction`, `current-bidi-paragraph-direction`, `bidi-resolved-levels`, `move-point-visually`, and `<left>` and `<right>` as `left-char` and `right-char`, by the paragraph's direction or with `visual-order-cursor-movement`).
- Invisible text and folding (`outline-mode`, `outline-minor-mode`), and hiding blocks of code and comments (`hs-minor-mode`: `C-c @ C-h`, `C-c @ C-s`, `C-c @ C-c`, `C-c @ C-M-h`, `C-c @ C-l`).
- Org IDs (`org-id-get-create`, `org-id-copy`, `org-id-goto`, and `[[id:...]]` links followed with `C-c C-o`), and link types for packages (`linkSetParameters`, as `org-link-set-parameters`).

- Narrowing (`C-x n n`, `C-x n w`).
- Tabs (`tab-bar-mode`: `C-x t 2`, `C-x t o`, `C-<tab>`, `C-x t 0`, `C-x t u`, `C-x t r`, `C-x t m`, `C-x t RET`, `C-x t b`, `C-x t f`, `C-x t d`, `tab-list`, clicks on the tab bar, and `tab-bar-history-mode` with `C-c <left>` and `C-c <right>`).
- Sessions (`desktop-save-mode`: buffers, their modes and places, `file-name-history` and the windows saved on exit and while idle, and read back at start; `desktop-save`, `desktop-read`, `desktop-clear`, `desktop-revert`, with a lock file as Emacs's, asking in the minibuffer at start whether to use a desktop another E8 has locked).
- Transient menus for packages (`transient.ts`: `transientDefinePrefix`, suffixes that stay, leave or return, switches and options read with `transientArgs`, options with several values, incompatible arguments, menus within menus, `C-g` and `C-q`; levels, `C-x l` to change them and `C-x a` to see them all, kept in `transient/levels.json`; `C-x s`, `C-x C-s` and `C-x C-k` to set, save and reset a menu's arguments, kept in `transient/values.json`; `C-x p` and `C-x n` for earlier arguments; `C-h` for a suffix's help; `C-x t` for the common commands; a menu taller than the frame is cut to fit, and `transient-show-popup` nil shows it only on `C-h`).

- Language servers (`M-x eglot`, `eglot-ensure`: definitions on `M-.`, references on `M-?`, back with `M-,`, completion on `C-M-i`, diagnostics underlined with `flymake-goto-next-error`, what point is on after a pause, `eglot-rename`, `eglot-format-buffer`, `eglot-code-actions`), and `xref-backend-functions` and `completion-at-point-functions` for other packages.
- Themes (`load-theme`, `enable-theme`, `disable-theme`, `custom-enabled-themes`): a theme is a package, NAME-theme, along `load-path`; both front ends draw in its colours, the GUI's mode line, header line, tab bar and cursor too, and on macOS its title bar, which takes the theme's background. `load-theme` completes over every theme along `load-path` (`custom-available-themes`) and loads a theme's file again each time, as Emacs does, so options set before it take effect; `e8.load` runs a package again for scripts that want the same. A face's `:distant-foreground` takes the place of its foreground when that is too near the background (`face-near-same-color-threshold`), and a whole-number `:height` factor is written `{ factor: 2 }`, as a whole number alone is an absolute height. `examples/packages/example-theme.ts` is a small one to write from, and 168 more are a package, [`e8-themes`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-themes) ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-themes-are-one-collection.md)).
- Indirect buffers (`C-x 4 c`, and Org's `C-c C-x b` for a subtree).
- Fields (C-a and the word commands stop at a prompt's edge).
- Tree-sitter highlighting (`rust-ts-mode`, `js-ts-mode`, `json-ts-mode`, `python-ts-mode`, and `go-ts-mode` and `bash-ts-mode`, which `sh-mode` and Org's `bash`, `sh` and `shell` blocks run, with a grammar library), loading the grammars Emacs builds (`libtree-sitter-LANG.dylib` along `treesit-extra-load-path` or in `~/.config/e8/tree-sitter`), and modes' `treesit-font-lock-rules` by `treesit-font-lock-level`.
- Indentation by Emacs's tree-sitter rules.
- Emacs Lisp mode (`emacs-lisp-mode` for `.el` files and Org's `emacs-lisp` and `elisp` blocks, `lisp-data-mode` for `.eld` and `.dir-locals.el`): indenting as Emacs does, by `lisp-indent-line`, `indent-region` and `C-M-q`, with Emacs's indent specs for its macros, and Emacs Lisp's faces, for doc strings, definitions, keywords, `:keywords` and `&optional`.

- Minibuffer history (`M-p`, `M-n`).
- The minibuffer (a buffer with its prompt as read-only text, `C-x o` out of it and back, and recursive minibuffers with `enable-recursive-minibuffers`).
- Help ([ADR 0064](adr/0064-the-help-system.md)): `C-h k`, `C-h c`, `C-h f`, `C-h x`, `C-h w`, `C-h b`, `C-h a` (apropos, with `apropos-variable` and `apropos`), `C-h o`, `C-h ?`, `C-h` after a prefix key, and `<f1>` as `C-h`; in `*Help*`, RET follows a name, TAB moves between them, and `l` and `r` go back and forward. Every text comes from E8's own definitions and keymaps.
- TypeScript extensions: `init.ts` defines commands and key bindings, `M-x load-file` reloads it, and `M-:` evaluates an expression.
- Packages from git repositories, as package-vc and use-package's `:vc` install them ([ADR 0059](adr/0059-packages-are-git-repositories.md)): `e8.usePackage("owner/name", { rev })` in `init.ts` installs a package into `repos/` beside `init.ts` at its newest version tag, or the revision given, puts it on `load-path` and requires it; `e8-lock.json` records each package's commit, so another machine installs the same, cloning every package it names at once; while `init.ts` installs them the window shows the editor and each message as it comes; `package-vc-install`, `package-vc-upgrade`, `package-vc-upgrade-all` (fetching in the background, all at once, and upgrading a requirement that is too old, first) and `package-delete`. A package's `e8-package.json` names it, the oldest E8 it runs on (`e8-version`) and the packages it requires.
- Batch mode, as `emacs --batch`: `e8 --batch -L DIR -l FILE -f FUNCTION --eval EXPRESSION`, windows laid out 80 by 24 as Emacs's are, with messages on standard error and `kill-emacs`'s status as the program's, and ERT (`ert.ts`: `ertDeftest`, `should`, `shouldEqual`, `shouldError`, `skipUnless`, `ert-run-tests-batch-and-exit`, `M-x ert`) with `e8.executeKbdMacro` to type keys, as `execute-kbd-macro`, so a package's repository runs its tests in CI. `e8 --print-types DIR` writes `e8.d.ts` and the shipped packages there, for `tsc` to check a package against, as E8 writes them beside `init.ts`.
- Ediff (`M-x ediff-files`, `ediff-buffers`, `ediff-files3`, `ediff-buffers3`, `ediff-merge-files`, `ediff-merge-buffers` and their `-with-ancestor` merges, with the aliases `ediff`, `ebuffers` and the rest): the buffers compared one above another, or side by side after `|`, with the control panel below, the differences from `diff` or `diff3` shaded, the current one highlighted and refined word by word; in the panel `n`, `p`, `SPC`, `DEL`, `j`, `ga`, `gb`, `a` and `b` to copy a region across (`ab`, `ba`, `ac` and the rest when comparing three), `ra` and `rb` to put back what a copy replaced, `wa`, `wb`, `wc`, `wd`, `!` to compare again after editing, `*`, `@`, `##`, `A`, `B`, `D`, `C-l`, `?` for every key and `q`; merging writes `*ediff-merge*`, taking what only one side changed and combining the rest, with `a`, `b`, `+`, `r`, `&`, `$$` and `$*`. Packages call it as magit-ediff.el does, with startup hooks that set its variables in the control panel (`import { ediffMergeBuffersWithAncestor } from "ediff"`, or `ediff-buffers-internal` by name). Loaded on first use ([ADR 0065](adr/0065-packages-emacs-autoloads.md)).
- Quick help for newcomers ([ADR 0068](adr/0068-quick-help-for-newcomers.md)), on top of Emacs's: `C-h h` shows a card about the mode you are in (what it is for, the keys that matter, what to try and to learn next, links to `C-h m`), `C-h 8` one on getting started with E8, and E8 says so at startup. Org, Dired, Prog mode and agent-shell have cards of their own; another mode's is made from its doc string and keys, on its parent's card. `M-x help-guide-for-mode` shows any mode's. A key init.ts gave to another command is still named, with what it runs now, as `C-x b … (now consult-buffer)`, from the keys remembered in `before-init-hook`. `help-guide-mode` turns it all off.

## Packages in their own repositories

Ports of packages from outside Emacs, in repositories of their own in `e8-editor`, each with its own tests, README and the upstream's licence and credit, installed with `e8.usePackage("e8-editor/e8-NAME")` as a config installs them from ELPA or MELPA in Emacs ([ADR 0059](adr/0059-packages-are-git-repositories.md)). Each README says where its port differs from the package it follows. E8's CI runs their tests against `main` (the `Ports` job).

- [`e8-vundo`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-vundo): the undo history as a tree (`M-x vundo`: `f`, `b`, `n`, `p`, `a`, `w`, `e`, `l`, `r`, `m`, `u`, `d` for a diff, `RET`, `q`), after vundo 2.4. `e8.usePackage("e8-editor/e8-vundo")`.
- [`e8-themes`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-themes): 168 themes, each under its upstream's licence and credited to its authors and the commit it was ported from: Modus and the forty Ef themes, sixty-three of Doom's, twenty-five from solarized-emacs (Selenized, Nightfox, Kanagawa, Everforest, Flexoki among them), fourteen of GNU Emacs's own, Gruvbox, Tomorrow, Catppuccin's flavours, Nord, Dracula and Zenburn, written from their upstreams by a script that reads the faces Emacs gives them, with repeats and hard-to-read themes left out ([ADR 0067](adr/0067-themes-are-one-collection.md)). `M-x e8-themes-select` lists them with a strip of each one's colours beside a sample of code, Org and a diff, showing each as the selection moves: `RET` keeps it, `q` goes back, `/` narrows the list as you type, `TAB` shows dark or light ones only. `e8.usePackage("e8-editor/e8-themes")`, then `e8.call("load-theme", "modus-vivendi", true)`.
- [`e8-persistent-scratch`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-persistent-scratch): the scratch buffers kept across sessions (`persistent-scratch-save`, `persistent-scratch-restore`, `persistent-scratch-autosave-mode`, `persistent-scratch-setup-default`, backups), in the file format persistent-scratch.el writes.
- [`e8-casual`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-casual): Casual's Dired menu (`casual-dired-tmenu`, usually on `?` in Dired), with its menus for sorting, links, changing files, marking by regexp, utilities, searching and settings, after Casual 2.
- [`e8-vertico`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-vertico): every prompt that completes lists its candidates down the minibuffer (`vertico-mode`: `C-n`, `C-p`, `<down>`, `<up>`, `M-<`, `M->`, `TAB`, `RET`, `M-RET`, groups with `M-{` and `M-}`, `vertico-count`, `vertico-cycle`, `vertico-preselect`, sorting by history, length and name), after vertico 2.5.
- [`e8-orderless`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-orderless): the completion style that matches space-separated components in any order, as literals or regexps, with orderless's affix characters and smart case (`completion-styles` `("orderless" "basic")`).
- [`e8-marginalia`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-marginalia): annotations beside completion candidates by category (`marginalia-mode`: commands' keys and documentation, variables' values, faces' samples, buffers, files' modes, sizes and ages, bookmarks, minor modes, themes, tabs), and `marginalia-cycle` (`M-A` in a config) to switch annotators.
- [`e8-consult`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-consult): commands that read with completion and preview where the selection is (`consult-line`, `consult-buffer` with its sources and `b`, `f`, `m`, `p`, `*` and `SPC` narrowing, `consult-buffer-other-window`, `consult-project-buffer`, `consult-recent-file`, `consult-bookmark`, `consult-goto-line`, `consult-outline`, `consult-imenu`, `consult-imenu-multi`, `consult-mark`, `consult-global-mark`, `consult-line-multi`, `consult-register`, `consult-register-load`, `consult-register-store`, `consult-isearch-history`, `consult-yank-pop`, `consult-yank-from-kill-ring`, `consult-history`, `consult-complex-command`, `consult-mode-command`, `consult-ripgrep`, `consult-grep` and `consult-find` searching as you type with `#search#filter`, `consult-compile-error`, `consult-flymake`, `consult-xref` for `xref-show-xrefs-function` and `xref-show-definitions-function`, `consult-theme`), `consult-narrow-key`, `consult-preview-key` and `consultCustomize`.
- [`e8-magit`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-magit), with [`e8-magit-section`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-magit-section): Git in a status buffer of sections (`C-x g`, `magit-status`: the branch, upstream, push remote and tag, untracked files, unstaged and staged changes as diffs, stashes, unpushed, unpulled and recent commits; `TAB`, `C-<tab>`, `n`, `p`, `M-n`, `M-p`, `^`, `1` to `4`; `s`, `u`, `k` on a file, hunk or section, `S`, `U`, `RET` to visit, `g`, `$`), with Magit's transient menus, laid out as Magit lays them out: commit (`c`: commit, extend, amend, reword, fixup, squash, alter, augment, revise and instant fixup and squash, the message written in `COMMIT_EDITMSG` with `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k`, git's own template and hooks through with-editor), push (`P`), pull (`F`), fetch (`f`), branch (`b`, with its configuration), merge (`m`), rebase (`r`: onto the upstream or elsewhere, interactively with the todo list in `git-rebase-mode`, to modify, reword or remove a commit, to autosquash, and continue, skip, edit and abort while one is going on), reset (`X`), stash (`z`), log (`l`, with reflogs and shortlog; `RET` shows a commit), diff (`d`) and dispatch (`h`, `?`: cherry-pick, revert, tag, rebase onto a branch, ignore, init, clone, refs, cherries, remotes, format-patch), after Magit 4; `s` and `u` with a region in a hunk take just its lines.
- [`e8-with-editor`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-with-editor): E8 as the editor of the programs it runs, as git's commit messages and rebase todo lists are edited in E8, with `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k`, through with-editor's sleeping editor.
- [`e8-org-roam`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-org-roam): notes as nodes, files and headings with IDs, in `org-roam-directory` (`org-roam-node-find`, `org-roam-node-insert`, `org-roam-capture` with `org-roam-capture-templates`, `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k` in the capture buffer, `org-roam-buffer-toggle` for the backlinks of the node at point, `org-roam-db-sync`, `org-roam-db-autosync-mode`, aliases, refs and tags, `org-roam-node-random`, `org-roam-graph` with Graphviz), after org-roam 2.
- [`e8-consult-org-roam`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-consult-org-roam): nodes read with consult's preview (`consult-org-roam-mode`), `consult-org-roam-backlinks`, `consult-org-roam-forward-links`, `consult-org-roam-file-find`, `consult-org-roam-search`, and the notes open as an Org-roam group of `consult-buffer`.
- [`e8-popper`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-popper): buffers named in `popper-reference-buffers`, by name, major mode or predicate, as popups that `popper-toggle` hides and shows again, the last first, and `popper-cycle` goes through, grouped by project with `popper-group-by-project`, with ` POP` in their mode line; `popper-toggle-type` makes a buffer a popup or a regular one, after popper 0.4.8.
