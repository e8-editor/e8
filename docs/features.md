# What E8 does

What works today, in a terminal (`e8`) or a window (`e8-gui`). Where it differs from Emacs is in [differences.md](differences.md), and what is still to come in [plan.md](plan.md).

- Buffers.
- Windows (`C-x 2`, `C-x 3`, `C-x o`, `C-x 0`, `C-x 1`, `split-window` and `delete-window` as window.el has them, with atomic windows, `delete-windows-on`, `delete-other-windows-vertically` and `window-toggle-side-windows`, resizing as window.el resizes, with `C-x ^`, `C-x }`, `C-x {`, `C-x +`, `C-x -`, `balance-windows-area`, `fit-window-to-buffer`, `maximize-window`, `minimize-window` and the mouse, preserved and fixed sizes, `display-buffer` as window.el has it, with `display-buffer-alist`, its action functions and side windows, and the `C-x 4` and `C-x 5` commands, `C-M-v`, `M-<next>`, `M-<prior>`, `M-<home>` and `M-<end>` in the next window, `rename-uniquely`, `C-x <left>` and `C-x <right>` through the buffers a window showed, `quit-window` restoring the window's buffer, place and size as window.el's `quit-restore-window` does, window parameters, dedicated windows, window states (`window-state-get`, `window-state-put`, `window-swap-states`), window configurations with `save-window-excursion`, moving, showing, deleting and swapping by direction with windmove (`windmove-left` and the rest, `windmove-default-keybindings` for `S-<left>` and its kin, `windmove-display-right` and its kin, `windmove-delete-*`, `windmove-swap-states-*`, `windmove-wrap-around`, `windmove-create-window`), and `winner-mode`, with `C-c <left>` and `C-c <right>` undoing and redoing changes to the windows).
- Frames (`C-x 5 2`, `C-x 5 o`, `C-x 5 0` and the other `C-x 5` commands, each frame a native window in the GUI).
- Files (backups, auto-saving and `recover-this-file`, `revert-buffer` and `C-x x g`, with `revert-buffer-function` and `buffer-stale-function`, `auto-revert-mode`, `auto-revert-tail-mode` and `global-auto-revert-mode` told of changes by kqueue or inotify, Dired and the buffer list reverted with `global-auto-revert-non-file-buffers`, asking before editing or saving a file changed on disk, DOS and Mac line ends, files in other codings (UTF-16, the ISO 8859 and Windows sets, Shift_JIS, EUC-JP, ISO-2022-JP, GBK, Big5, EUC-KR, found from cookies and the bytes), `C-x RET f`, `C-x RET r` and `C-x RET c`, `describe-coding-system` on `C-h C`, `prefer-coding-system`, `save-some-buffers` and asking about each file on `C-x C-c`, `copy-file`, `rename-file`, `delete-file` to the trash, `make-directory`, `read-only-mode` on `C-x C-q`, visiting a file read-only with `C-x C-r`, `C-x 4 r` and `C-x 5 r`, `find-alternate-file` on `C-x C-v`, `rename-visited-file`, recent files with `recentf-mode` and `recentf-open`, and `find-file-at-point`, `insert-file` on `C-x i`, `write-region`, `append-to-file`, and lock files, the umask with `default-file-modes` and `with-file-modes`, `verify-visited-file-modtime`, `file-name-completion` and `file-name-all-completions`, `directory-files-and-attributes` and `access-file`, and the primitives files.el builds on: `lock-file` and `lock-buffer`, the `-internal` operations, `file-system-info` and `unix-sync`), gzip and zlib data decompressed in a buffer with `zlib-decompress-region`, and `symbol-file` naming the file that defined a function, variable or face. TLS on network connections, as Emacs's GnuTLS gives it (`gnutls-boot`, `gnutls-peer-status`), started at once or after plain text as STARTTLS does, with `open-network-stream`'s `:type tls` and the Network Security Manager asking about certificates that do not verify or keys that changed, remembered for the session or for good; `secure-hash` and `md5`, and GnuTLS's hashes, MACs and symmetric ciphers (`gnutls-hash-mac` for SCRAM authentication, `gnutls-symmetric-encrypt`).
- Dired (`C-x d`, `C-x C-j`: visiting, marking, copying, renaming and deleting files, `g`, `s`, hiding details), with dired-aux's commands: links (`S`, `Y`, `H`), renaming and copying by regexp (`% R`, `% C` and the link ones), `% u` and `% l`, `% g` to mark files by what they hold, `!` and `&` with `*` and `?` and the commands they guess for the files, `y`, `M`, `O`, `G`, `T`, `Z` to compress and unpack, `A` to search the marked files into `*xref*` and `Q` to replace in them, with `Y` and `N` and `M-x fileloop-continue`, failures logged in `*Dired log*`; dired-x's `dired-omit-mode` (`C-x M-o`) and `F`; and wdired, `C-x C-q`, to rename files, point links elsewhere and with `wdired-allow-to-change-permissions` set their modes by editing the listing, searching only the names (`C-c C-c` to finish, `C-c ESC` or `C-c C-k` to take it back).
- Projects (`C-x p f` to find a file in the Git tree, `C-x p p`, `C-x p D`, `C-x p b`, `C-x p k`).
- Questions (`yes-or-no-p`, `y-or-n-p`, and `use-short-answers`) and timers.
- An agent (`M-x agent`: talk to Claude in an Org buffer while it calls commands as tools, asking before it writes files or quits, each turn one undo step).
- Coding agents over the Agent Client Protocol (`M-x agent-shell`, after Emacs's agent-shell: Claude Code through `claude-agent-acp`, Gemini CLI, Codex or Goose, in the same kind of Org buffer, its Markdown replies made Org as they stream in and drawn as a chat over that text: the turns as labels, each tool call with its status's mark (● done, ◔ running, × failed) and what it does, an edit with the lines it changed (+2 -1), code on a panel under its language, the agent's thinking and tool calls between what it says gathered in a line that says what was done, "Thought, ran 2 commands", which folds once it goes on (TAB opens it), and a spinner while it works, `C-c RET` to send and `C-c C-k` to stop, its session started as the buffer opens with its model, mode and thought level on the header line (`agent-shell-header-style`, and in variables for one's own mode line), `C-c C-v` to pick the model, `C-c C-t` the thought level, `C-c TAB` or `C-<tab>` the next mode and `C-c C-s` any setting it offers, `C-h h` for its keys, and whether it is working and how full its context is on the mode line; the agent's tool calls, diffs and plan show as it works, each call's output in a src block named by what it holds (diff, console, json or text) folded into the call's line as it comes (TAB opens one, and a call asked about shows its diff while it asks), its thinking a `#+begin_thinking` block folded as any Org block, its tool lines short (commands with their description, MCP tools as `server: tool`, Claude Code's bookkeeping left out) Org's emphasis markers hidden, and the conversation above the request being typed read-only, as a shell's, undo reaching only that request, `M-p` and `M-n` bringing the requests sent before back to the prompt and `M-r` picking one with completion, from one history every agent-shell buffer shares and `savehist-mode` keeps between sessions, earlier sessions in the project resumed as it opens (asked, or the latest with `agent-shell-session-strategy`; Claude Code's own sessions there among them), their conversation replayed into the buffer, every reply but the last folded, and the agent remembering it, the agent's slash commands (Claude Code's own, skills and plugins) completed with TAB or `C-M-i` after a `/` at the start of a request, with what each does in the echo area, an agent that stops started again in the same session, Org's speed commands running anywhere in the read-only transcript (`n`, `p`, `f`, `b`, `u`, `c`, `C`, `o`, `?`), its permission questions are asked when nothing else is asking, files it reads and writes through the editor go through their buffers, and buffers of files it wrote itself are reverted; its login is its own; it also gets E8's own commands, keys and buffers through `e8-mcp`, asking before it saves, discards, quits or runs code in the editor).
- Emacs movement and editing keys (`M-m`, `M-\`, `M-SPC`, `M-^`, `C-x C-o`, `M-z`, `C-q`, `C-S-DEL`, `M-g g`, `M-g TAB` (`move-to-column`), `C-x =`, `M-=`, `M-@`, `M-h`, `C-M-o`, sentences with `M-a`, `M-e` and `M-k`, `sort-lines`, `reverse-region`, `delete-duplicate-lines`, `keep-lines`, `flush-lines` and `how-many`, registers (`C-x r SPC`, `C-x r j`, `C-x r s`, `C-x r i`) and rectangles (`C-x r k`, `C-x r y`, `C-x r t`), `M-/` (`dabbrev-expand`), abbrevs (`abbrev-mode`, `C-x a g`, `C-x '`, a mode's abbrevs working in modes built on it, kept in `abbrev_defs` in Emacs's format, read at startup and offered for saving by `save-some-buffers` and on quitting), `M-s o` (`occur`, and `multi-occur`, `multi-occur-in-matching-buffers` and the buffer list's `M-s a C-o` over several buffers), pages (`C-x ]`, `C-x [`, `C-x l`), `C-M-t`, `C-M-h`, `C-x n d`, `C-l` and `M-r` cycling as `recenter-top-bottom` and `move-to-window-line-top-bottom`, the global mark ring (`C-x C-SPC`), `undo-only`, `ESC ESC ESC`, the buffer list (`C-x C-b`, a tabulated list sorted with `S`, titled in the header line), `kill-some-buffers` and `kill-matching-buffers`, highlighting by regexp (`M-s h r`, `M-s h .`), bookmarks (`C-x r m`, `C-x r b`, `C-x r l`, in Emacs's file format), `whitespace-cleanup`, `view-mode` and `view-file`, `M-x diff`, `diff-buffer-with-file` and `diff-mode`, `tabify`, `center-line` and `append-to-buffer`, changing case with `M-u`, `M-l`, `M-c`, `C-x C-u` and `C-x C-l`, paragraphs with `M-{` and `M-}`, defuns with `C-M-a` and `C-M-e` (by the syntax tree in tree-sitter modes), balanced expressions with `C-M-f`, `C-M-b`, `C-M-n`, `C-M-p`, `C-M-u`, `C-M-d`, `C-M-k`, `C-M-SPC` and `M-(`, as each mode's syntax table says).
- Filling (`M-q`, `C-u M-q` to justify, `fill-region`, `C-x f`, `C-x .`, `auto-fill-mode`; a comment filled as a comment, each line starting with its comment start; in programming modes `M-q` fills the comment or string point is in and otherwise reindents the function). Paragraphs as `paragraph-start`, `paragraph-separate` and `fill-prefix` say (`M-}`, `M-{`, `M-h`).
- Matching parens shown (`show-paren-mode`, on by default) and typed in pairs (`electric-pair-mode`).
- Line numbers (`display-line-numbers-mode`, `global-display-line-numbers-mode`).
- History and places kept between sessions (`savehist-mode`, `save-place-mode`).
- A shell in a buffer (`M-x shell`, on comint: `M-p`, `M-n`, `M-r` searching the history with isearch, `C-c M-r`, the shell's history file, `C-c C-c`, `C-c C-o`, the directory followed, passwords read hidden with `read-passwd`, `TAB` completing file names, commands and environment variables, `C-c C-f` and `C-c C-b` over the commands of a line, ANSI colours as Emacs's ansi-color makes them, and `comint-use-prompt-regexp` for programs whose prompts fields cannot mark).
- Shell commands (`M-!`, `M-|`, `M-&`).
- The environment programs run in, as Emacs keeps it ([ADR 0112](adr/0112-the-environment-programs-run-in.md)): `process-environment` and `initial-environment`, `M-x setenv` and `M-x getenv`, `substitute-env-vars`, `with-environment-variables`, programs found along `exec-path`, and `call-process` as Emacs takes it, with an input file and DISPLAY. Network connections made without waiting (`:nowait`), datagrams (UDP, `:type datagram`, `process-datagram-address`), and connections and pipe processes stopped and continued (`:stop`, `stop-process`).
- Going to a definition by name (`M-x imenu`, from the syntax tree in tree-sitter modes, Python's included, and the headings in Org; the index kept until `*Rescan*` or made afresh with `imenu-auto-rescan`, flattened with `imenu-flatten`, and put on the menu bar with `imenu-add-menubar-index`).
- Compiling and grepping in the background (`M-x compile`, `M-x grep`, `recompile`, `C-x p c`) and visiting each message's place (`next-error` on `M-g n`, `M-g p`).
- Finding a regexp in a project (`C-x p g`, into `*xref*`).
- Comments (`M-;`, `C-x C-;`, `C-x ;`, `M-j`).
- Indentation (TAB as the mode says, `C-M-\` through `indent-region-function` where the mode has one, as Org mode's `org-indent-region`, `C-x TAB`, RET and closing braces reindenting in code modes).
- The message log (`C-h e` shows `*Messages*`).
- Warnings (`display-warning`, `warn`, `lwarn`): logged in `*Warnings*` and shown, as `warning-minimum-level`, `warning-minimum-log-level`, `warning-suppress-types` and `warning-suppress-log-types` say, the ⛔ button asking to suppress a type.
- The active region with shift selection (transient-mark-mode and shift-select-mode, both on).
- Prefix arguments (`C-u`, `M-<digit>`, `C--`).
- Mouse wheel and trackpad scrolling of the window under the pointer.
- Clicks.
- Drags.
- Double and triple clicks and shift-click as Emacs mouse keys (`<mouse-1>`, `<drag-mouse-1>`, `<double-mouse-1>`).
- Windows' places on the frame (`window-pixel-left`, `window-pixel-top`, `window-left-column`, `window-top-line`), fringes of their own (`set-window-fringes`, which the window obeys), a cursor of their own (`set-window-cursor-type`), what they were at the last redisplay (`window-old-buffer`, `window-old-point`, `old-selected-window`), and window configurations compared (`window-configuration-frame`, `window-configuration-equal-p`).
- Terminals as objects (`terminal-list`, `terminal-live-p`, `frame-terminal`, `terminal-name`, `terminal-parameter`, `set-terminal-parameter`, `delete-terminal`, `tty-type`, `tty-display-color-p`, `controlling-tty-p`, `suspend-tty`, `resume-tty`): one terminal, which every frame is on.
- Frames' geometry (`frame-text-width`, `frame-native-width`, `frame-position`, `set-frame-position`, `set-frame-size`, `frame-parameters`, `modify-frame-parameters`, the widths around a frame's text), where a frame's keys go (`redirect-frame-focus`, `frame-focus`), `lower-frame` and `x-parse-geometry`.
- Dialogs and tooltips (`x-popup-dialog`, `message-box`, `message-or-box`, `use-dialog-box-p`, `x-show-tip`, `x-hide-tip`, `tooltip-mode`, `x-select-font`), drawn as menus are, in a terminal as in the window, as Emacs emulates a dialog with a menu where the display has none.
- A menu bar (`menu-bar-mode`, off until you turn it on): File, Edit, Options, Buffers, Tools and Help as `menu-bar.el` has them, a mode's own menus from its keymap's `[menu-bar]` bindings, `F10` to open it, the arrows and `RET` to walk it and a click to open the menu under the pointer, in a terminal as in the window; `easymenu.ts` for writing a mode's menu as data, and `menu-bar-final-items` and `menu-bar-update-hook` as Emacs has them.
- Pop-up menus (`x-popup-menu`): menu keymaps whose bindings are `menu-item`s, with submenus, `:enable`, `:visible`, `:help`, `:filter` and radio and toggle buttons, drawn over the windows in a terminal as in the GUI, walked with the arrows, `RET`, `C-g` and the mouse as Emacs's terminal menus are, and `menu-or-popup-active-p`.
- The pointer's place and moving it (`mouse-position`, `mouse-pixel-position`, `set-mouse-position`), `track-mouse` with `<mouse-movement>` events, the window and the part of it at a place (`window-at`, `coordinates-in-window-p`), and the raw keys of a key sequence (`this-single-command-raw-keys`, `read-key-sequence-vector`).
- Long lines continued by default.
- Who runs E8 and on what, for scripts as Emacs's Lisp asks it (`user-full-name`, `user-login-name`, ids, `system-users`, `num-processors`), and `C-z` and `C-x C-z` (`suspend-frame`) iconifying a window or stopping the terminal for the shell, as `suspend-emacs` does.
- `truncate-lines`, `visual-line-mode` and `global-visual-line-mode`, and horizontal scrolling as Emacs's: long truncated lines scroll sideways to keep point in sight (`auto-hscroll-mode`, `hscroll-margin`, `hscroll-step`), `C-x <` and `C-x >` (`scroll-left`, `scroll-right`), and `window-hscroll` and `set-window-hscroll` for scripts, with `window-vscroll` and `set-window-vscroll`, which the GUI's smooth scrolling sets, `vertical-motion`, `compute-motion`, `line-number-display-width`, `window-line-height`, `window-lines-pixel-dimensions`, `other-window-for-scrolling` and `window-scroll-functions`.
- File-local variables, as Emacs's files.el sets them: a `-*-` line (`-*- mode: org; fill-column: 60 -*-`, on the second line after `#!`) and a Local Variables list near the end of the file, with its prefix and suffix, as `# Local Variables:` ... `# End:`; `mode:` choosing the major mode; safe values set at once and unsafe or risky ones asked about in `*Local Variables*` (`y`, `n`, `!`, `i`), as `enable-local-variables`, `safe-local-variable-values`, the `safe-local-variable` and `risky-local-variable` properties and `enable-local-eval` say; `eval:` for what E8 can run; `file-local-variables-alist`, `hack-local-variables-hook` and `before-hack-local-variables-hook`; and the variables set again whenever the major mode changes. `add-file-local-variable`, `delete-file-local-variable`, `add-file-local-variable-prop-line` and `delete-file-local-variable-prop-line` write them. Directory-local variables from `.dir-locals.el` (and `.dir-locals-2.el`, which overrides it) in the file's directory or one above it, by major mode, derived modes included, and by subdirectory, with `subdirs`, read again when the file changes, asked about as file-local ones are with `+` to trust the directory (`safe-local-variable-directories`), in Dired buffers too, and set for code with `dir-locals-set-class-variables` and `dir-locals-set-directory-class`; `add-dir-local-variable`, `delete-dir-local-variable`, `copy-file-locals-to-dir-locals`, `copy-dir-locals-to-file-locals` and `copy-dir-locals-to-file-locals-prop-line` write them. Scripts read and print Lisp data with `e8.readFromString` and `e8.prin1ToString`.
- Spell checking with aspell, ispell, hunspell or enchant-2, as Emacs's ispell.el: `M-$` (`ispell-word`, corrections in `*Choices*`: a character picks one, `SPC` leaves the word, `i`, `u` and `m` add to your personal dictionary, `a` accepts for the session, `A` in the file's `LocalWords:`, `r` and `R` type a replacement, `R` query-replacing it through the buffer, `l` looks words up in a word list, `x`, `X` and `q` stop, `C-r` edits recursively, `?` helps), `ispell-region`, `ispell-buffer`, `ispell` and `ispell-continue` (`C-u M-$`), skipping URLs, `LocalWords:` lines, HTML markup (`ispell-skip-html`), TeX commands and, with `ispell-check-comments`, comments; `ispell-comments-and-strings`, `ispell-comment-or-string-at-point`, `ispell-complete-word` and word completion at point in Text mode from a word list, `ispell-minor-mode`, `ispell-kill-ispell` and `ispell-pdict-save`. Dictionaries are found as Emacs finds them (`ispell-valid-dictionary-list`) and chosen with `ispell-change-dictionary` (`C-u` for every buffer), `ispell-dictionary`, or a file's own `Local IspellDict:` and `Local IspellPersDict:` lines, or `ispell-local-dictionary` as a file-local or directory-local variable. Several languages at once need Hunspell: set `ispell-program-name` to `hunspell` and `ispell-dictionary` to the dictionaries joined by commas, as `en_US,sv_SE`, and words either knows are right. `flyspell-mode` checks words as you type, underlining misspellings and doubled words; `C-.`/`M-TAB` cycle corrections, `C-;` corrects the misspelling before point, `C-,` goes to the next, `C-c $` offers corrections; `flyspell-prog-mode`, `flyspell-buffer` and `flyspell-region`.
- `subword-mode` (the word commands stop inside CamelCase names) and `superword-mode` (a symbol is one word), and their global modes.
- The kill ring with `M-y` (choosing a kill from the minibuffer when not after a yank).
- The mark ring (`C-u C-SPC`).
- Undo as in Emacs (undoable, with `undo-redo`, text property changes undone, `buffer-undo-list` read, set and bound, `with-silent-modifications`).
- Keyboard macros (`F3`, `F4`, `C-x (`, `C-x )`, `C-x e`).
- Recursive edits (`C-M-c`, `C-]`, `top-level`).

- `sit-for` and `read-event` with a timeout.

- Documented variables with buffer-local values (`C-h v`).
- Hooks.

- Header lines (`header-line-format`).
- Margins (`left-margin-width`, `(margin left-margin)` display specs) and fringes in the GUI (continuation arrows, `(left-fringe BITMAP)` specs, `define-fringe-bitmap`, `indicate-empty-lines`, `indicate-buffer-boundaries`, `fringe-indicator-alist`).

- [`e8-magit`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-magit), with [`e8-magit-section`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-magit-section): Git in a status buffer of sections (`C-x g`, `magit-status`: the branch, upstream, push remote and tag, untracked files, unstaged and staged changes as diffs, stashes, unpushed, unpulled and recent commits; `TAB`, `C-<tab>`, `n`, `p`, `M-n`, `M-p`, `^`, `1` to `4`; `s`, `u`, `k` on a file, hunk or section, `S`, `U`, `RET` to visit, `g`, `$`), with Magit's transient menus, laid out as Magit lays them out: commit (`c`: commit, extend, amend, reword, fixup, squash, alter, augment, revise and instant fixup and squash, the message written in `COMMIT_EDITMSG` with `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k`, git's own template and hooks through with-editor), push (`P`), pull (`F`), fetch (`f`), branch (`b`, with its configuration), merge (`m`), rebase (`r`: onto the upstream or elsewhere, interactively with the todo list in `git-rebase-mode`, to modify, reword or remove a commit, to autosquash, and continue, skip, edit and abort while one is going on), reset (`X`), stash (`z`), log (`l`, with reflogs and shortlog; `RET` shows a commit), diff (`d`) and dispatch (`h`, `?`: cherry-pick, revert, tag, rebase onto a branch, ignore, init, clone, refs, cherries, remotes, format-patch), after Magit 4; `s` and `u` with a region in a hunk take just its lines.
- [`e8-with-editor`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-with-editor): E8 as the editor of the programs it runs, as git's commit messages and rebase todo lists are edited in E8, with `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k`, through with-editor's sleeping editor.
- [`e8-org-roam`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-org-roam): notes as nodes, files and headings with IDs, in `org-roam-directory` (`org-roam-node-find`, `org-roam-node-insert`, `org-roam-capture` with `org-roam-capture-templates`, `C-c C-c` and `C-c C-k` in the capture buffer, `org-roam-buffer-toggle` for the backlinks of the node at point, `org-roam-db-sync`, `org-roam-db-autosync-mode`, aliases, refs and tags, `org-roam-node-random`, `org-roam-graph` with Graphviz), after org-roam 2.
- [`e8-consult-org-roam`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-consult-org-roam): nodes read with consult's preview (`consult-org-roam-mode`), `consult-org-roam-backlinks`, `consult-org-roam-forward-links`, `consult-org-roam-file-find`, `consult-org-roam-search`, and the notes open as an Org-roam group of `consult-buffer`.
- [`e8-popper`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-popper): buffers named in `popper-reference-buffers`, by name, major mode or predicate, as popups that `popper-toggle` hides and shows again, the last first, and `popper-cycle` goes through, grouped by project with `popper-group-by-project`, with ` POP` in their mode line; `popper-toggle-type` makes a buffer a popup or a regular one, after popper 0.4.8.
- [`e8-markdown-mode`](https://github.com/e8-editor/e8-markdown-mode): Markdown, and GitHub Flavored Markdown in `gfm-mode`, for `.md` files and the rest: headings, emphasis, code spans and fenced and indented blocks (in their language's faces with `markdown-fontify-code-blocks-natively`), links, footnotes, lists, checkboxes, quotes, tables, HTML and front matter styled, markup and URLs hidden with `C-c C-x C-m` and `C-c C-x C-l`; `TAB` and `S-TAB` folding headings, `C-c C-n`, `C-c C-p`, `C-c C-f`, `C-c C-b` and `C-c C-u` between them, `C-c C--` and `C-c C-=` promoting and demoting headings and list items, `C-c <up>` and `C-c <down>` moving them; `C-c C-s` inserting markup, headings, code blocks, rules, checkboxes and footnotes, `C-c C-l` links, `M-RET` list items; `C-c C-o` following links, `C-c C-d` ticking checkboxes, `M-n` and `M-p` between links, and `M-x imenu`; after markdown-mode 2.9. `e8.usePackage("e8-editor/e8-markdown-mode")`.
